WHAT THE MASK DOES

A soft preference, not a cut-out

The pilot combines valid-flow confidence with a calibrated ray-ground prior, then floors reliability at 0.25. Attention is downweighted; original max pooling remains active. No hard cell rejection is enabled.

HOW TO READ THIS

Attention is a clue

Bright cells carry more spatial attention. Comparing separately trained models shows where their weights differ; it does not isolate why a prediction changed. Ground-ray support is a hypothetical plane intersection, not a floor detector.

ONE SEED · 500 STEPS

What this pilot establishes

Yaw-local improves fixed-history-24 path error over the matched trained reference, but fails the history-selection gate. Masking worsens H24 ADE within both integration modes. Episode windows can behave differently.

Fair comparison limits

All four pilot arms reset their motion and uncertainty heads and train for 500 steps. The original checkpoint retains its trained heads and comes from a different budget. One seed does not establish generalization. The locked test has not been evaluated in this review.
