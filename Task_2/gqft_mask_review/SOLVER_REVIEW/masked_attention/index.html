Masked-attention images · historical experiments

62 PNGs from the October neural masking studies. These are different from the November geometric solver's central-floor pixel selection and from camera-height reconstruction.

Three experiments remain separate.

1. Frozen hard masks (13 October): reject late CNN pooling cells, affecting both attention and max branches.
2. Frozen soft reliability (15 October): retain the original max branch; validation gains are tiny and uncertain.
3. Trained soft-mask pilot (19 October): confidence/ground reliability with first24 flow pairs of episode629 legs0/2/3/4. These are short windows, not complete legs. The mask-only pilot regressed H24 ADE12.46→12.83px; mask+yaw-local regressed versus yaw-local11.35→11.81px.
