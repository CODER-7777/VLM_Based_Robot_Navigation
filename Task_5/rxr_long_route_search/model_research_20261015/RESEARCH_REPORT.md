# School backpack model research

Screened 54 public model records; curated 19 options. Downloaded 2 new CC-BY scans from the official AllenAI Objaverse distribution.

- [School Bag / Backpack Photoscan](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/school-bag-backpack-photoscan-64249ba38f5045bdaabbb5749aa30033): Login required. Closest free school-bag candidate. Official download requires a Sketchfab login/token; not downloaded.
- [Backpack Scan -  Free Game assets](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/backpack-scan-free-game-assets-708a2854348b46c7aa0fd9af55fe76a5): Downloaded. Xtreme-brand backpack scanned and repaired in Maya/Substance by its author. Public GLB downloaded for inspection; not placed in the scene.
- [Classic School Bag · 3DTRIXS](https://3dtrixs.com/3d-model/school-bag/): Free checkout. Public listing; the mesh has not been downloaded. 4K and format details are seller claims.
- [Safari School Backpack · 3DTRIXS](https://3dtrixs.com/3d-model/safari-school-backpack/): Free checkout. No card needed is advertised. Mesh not downloaded; seller 4K and format claims not independently inspected.
- [School Backpack](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/school-backpack-524d7fc724094ead9f203ed06bae76e1): Login required. Author used Marvelous Designer and Substance Painter. Mesh not downloaded; official download requires login.
- [Backpack Photoscan](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/backpack-photoscan-bbbb1521eb0a4c0296cf515503efb5ca): Login required. Author advertises a 4K diffuse and normal map. Public preview inspected; mesh not downloaded.
