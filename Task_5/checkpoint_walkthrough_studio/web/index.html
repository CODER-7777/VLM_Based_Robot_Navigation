INSTRUCTIONS FOR NEW SAMPLE SUBMISSIONS

Include and save your reasoning text

↓ Reasoning template TXT

Each submitted sample should include a written explanation of your decisions. Voice audio alone does not supply this text; automatic transcription is not enabled. You can also submit text only, without recording audio or video.

  1. Open Add your reasoning text, voice, or a new recording below. Enter your name and press New sample.
  2. Press Insert reasoning template, then replace the placeholders with your own observations and a brief action justification. Add an entry at important checkpoints or when your decision changes.
  3. Press Save reasoning text while working. When finished, press Stop & save sample and download the sample ZIP.

Your reasoning text is saved as transcript.txt and in manifest.json inside your sample ZIP. Existing walkthrough and checkpoint downloads do not require a new sample.

Show an example entry (illustrative only)

Checkpoint / time: Cxx / mm:ss
Task: Bring Riya’s school bag.
Observation: I can see a pink chair, but cannot yet confirm whether a school bag is on it.
Memory / constraint: For this example only, the supplied memory says the bag was left on that chair.
Next action: Approach the chair and inspect its seat.
Brief reason: The supplied memory points to this chair, but I need a closer view to identify the bag.
Uncertainty: The bag’s presence and identity are not confirmed.
Result: Fill this in after inspecting. Replace this example with the evidence and memory from your actual sample.
