# Speak while navigating

Use **Record screen + microphone**. Explain your decisions aloud as you navigate. Your voice and screen are recorded together.

Say what you see, which memory it matches, and why you choose the next action. For example:

- “I am turning left because [visible landmark] matches [the relevant memory].”
- “I am moving forward because [evidence that this is the correct passage].”
- “This does not match the memory. I am changing direction because [reason].”
- “I am stopping because [matching features] confirm the destination.”

Use your actual task, memories and current view. Speak during the run, rather than only giving a plan before moving.

After **STOP & save**, put what you said into **Corrected reasoning transcript** and click **Save reasoning text**.
